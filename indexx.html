Backtesting Engine Design
How do you build a backtester that doesn't lie to you?
After three iterations of a Python backtesting system, I keep hitting the same wall: look-ahead bias. It's subtle — your pipeline peeks into the future without you realising it, and your equity curve looks beautiful until real money is on the line.
